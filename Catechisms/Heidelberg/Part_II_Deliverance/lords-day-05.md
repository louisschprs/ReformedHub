## Lord’s Day 5

### Q & A 12

**Q. According to God’s righteous judgment we deserve punishment both now and in eternity: how then can we escape this punishment and return to God’s favor?**

**A.** God requires that his justice be satisfied.^[Ex. 23:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ex.%2023:7&version=nrsv); [Rom. 2:1-11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%202:1-11&version=nrsv) Therefore the claims of this justice must be paid in full, either by ourselves or by another.^[Isa. 53:11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isa.%2053:11&version=nrsv); [Rom. 8:3-4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%208:3-4&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 13

**Q. Can we make this payment ourselves?**

**A.** Certainly not. Actually, we increase our debt every day.^[Matt. 6:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matt.%206:12&version=nrsv); [Rom. 2:4-5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%202:4-5&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 14

**Q. Can another creature—any at all— pay this debt for us?**

**A.** No. To begin with, God will not punish any other creature for what a human is guilty of.^[Ezek. 18:4, 20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ezek.%2018:4,%2020&version=nrsv); [Heb. 2:14-18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Heb.%202:14-18&version=nrsv) Furthermore, no mere creature can bear the weight of God’s eternal wrath against sin and deliver others from it.^[Ps. 49:7-9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%2049:7-9&version=nrsv); [130:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%20130:3&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 15

**Q. What kind of mediator and deliverer should we look for then?**

**A.** One who is a true^[Rom. 1:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%201:3&version=nrsv); [1 Cor. 15:21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Cor.%2015:21&version=nrsv); [Heb. 2:17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Heb.%202:17&version=nrsv) and righteous^[Isa. 53:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isa.%2053:9&version=nrsv); [2 Cor. 5:21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Cor.%205:21&version=nrsv); [Heb. 7:26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Heb.%207:26&version=nrsv) human, yet more powerful than all creatures, that is, one who is also true God.^[Isa. 7:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isa.%207:14&version=nrsv); [9:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isa.%209:6&version=nrsv); [Jer. 23:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jer.%2023:6&version=nrsv); [John 1:1](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%201:1&version=nrsv)
