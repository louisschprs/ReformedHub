## Lord’s Day 1

### Q & A 1

**Q. What is your only comfort in life and in death?**

**A.** That I am not my own,^[1 Cor. 6:19-20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Cor.%206:19-20&version=nrsv) but belong—

body and soul, in life and in death—^[Rom. 14:7-9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%2014:7-9&version=nrsv)

to my faithful Savior, Jesus Christ.^[1 Cor. 3:23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Cor.%203:23&version=nrsv); [Titus 2:14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Titus%202:14&version=nrsv)

He has fully paid for all my sins with his precious blood,^[1 Pet. 1:18-19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Pet.%201:18-19&version=nrsv); [1 John 1:7-9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20John%201:7-9&version=nrsv); [2:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20John%202:2&version=nrsv) and has set me free from the tyranny of the devil.^[John 8:34-36](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%208:34-36&version=nrsv); [Heb. 2:14-15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Heb.%202:14-15&version=nrsv); [1 John 3:1-11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20John%203:1-11&version=nrsv) He also watches over me in such a way^[John 6:39-40](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%206:39-40&version=nrsv); [10:27-30](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%2010:27-30&version=nrsv); [2 Thess. 3:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Thess.%203:3&version=nrsv); [1 Pet. 1:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Pet.%201:5&version=nrsv) that not a hair can fall from my head without the will of my Father in heaven;^[Matt. 10:29-31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matt.%2010:29-31&version=nrsv); [Luke 21:16-18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Luke%2021:16-18&version=nrsv) in fact, all things must work together for my salvation.^[Rom. 8:28](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%208:28&version=nrsv)

Because I belong to him, Christ, by his Holy Spirit, assures me of eternal life^[Rom. 8:15-16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%208:15-16&version=nrsv); [2 Cor. 1:21-22](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Cor.%201:21-22&version=nrsv); [5:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Cor.%205:5&version=nrsv); [Eph. 1:13-14](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Eph.%201:13-14&version=nrsv) and makes me wholeheartedly willing and ready from now on to live for him.^[Rom. 8:1-17](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%208:1-17&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 2

**Q. What must you know to live and die in the joy of this comfort?**

**A.** Three things:

first, how great my sin and misery are;^[Rom. 3:9-10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%203:9-10&version=nrsv); [1 John 1:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20John%201:10&version=nrsv) second, how I am set free from all my sins and misery;^[John 17:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%2017:3&version=nrsv); [Acts 4:12](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%204:12&version=nrsv); [10:43](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Acts%2010:43&version=nrsv) third, how I am to thank God for such deliverance.^[Matt. 5:16](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matt.%205:16&version=nrsv); [Rom. 6:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%206:13&version=nrsv); [Eph. 5:8-10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Eph.%205:8-10&version=nrsv); [2 Tim. 2:15](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=2%20Tim.%202:15&version=nrsv); [1 Pet. 2:9-10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Pet.%202:9-10&version=nrsv)
