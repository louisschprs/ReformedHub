## Lord’s Day 2

### Q & A 3

**Q. How do you come to know your misery?**

**A.** The law of God tells me.^[Rom. 3:20](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%203:20&version=nrsv); [7:7-25](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%207:7-25&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 4

**Q. What does God’s law require of us?**

**A.** Christ teaches us this in summary in [Matthew 22:37-40](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matthew%2022:37-40&version=nrsv):

“You shall love the Lord your God  
with all your heart,  
and with all your soul,  
and with all your mind.”^[Deut. 6:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deut.%206:5&version=nrsv)  
This is the greatest and first commandment.

“And a second is like it:  
“You shall love your neighbor as yourself.”^[Lev. 19:18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Lev.%2019:18&version=nrsv)

“On these two commandments hang  
all the law and the prophets.”

### Q & A 5

**Q. Can you live up to all this perfectly?**

**A.** No.^[Rom. 3:9-20, 23](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%203:9-20,%2023&version=nrsv); [1 John 1:8, 10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20John%201:8,%2010&version=nrsv)  
I have a natural tendency  
to hate God and my neighbor.^[Gen. 6:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%206:5&version=nrsv); [Jer. 17:9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Jer.%2017:9&version=nrsv); [Rom. 7:23-24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%207:23-24&version=nrsv); [8:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%208:7&version=nrsv); [Eph. 2:1-3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Eph.%202:1-3&version=nrsv); [Titus 3:3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Titus%203:3&version=nrsv)
