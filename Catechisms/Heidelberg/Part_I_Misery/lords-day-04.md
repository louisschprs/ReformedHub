## Lord’s Day 4

### Q & A 9

**Q. But doesn’t God do us an injustice by requiring in his law what we are unable to do?**

**A.** No, God created human beings with the ability to keep the law.^[Gen. 1:31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%201:31&version=nrsv); [Eph. 4:24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Eph.%204:24&version=nrsv) They, however, provoked by the devil,^[Gen. 3:13](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%203:13&version=nrsv); [John 8:44](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%208:44&version=nrsv) in willful disobedience,^[Gen. 3:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%203:6&version=nrsv) robbed themselves and all their descendants of these gifts.^[Rom. 5:12, 18, 19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%205:12,%2018,%2019&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 10

**Q. Does God permit such disobedience and rebellion to go unpunished?**

**A.** Certainly not. God is terribly angry with the sin we are born with as well as the sins we personally commit. As a just judge, God will punish them both now and in eternity,^[Ex. 34:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ex.%2034:7&version=nrsv); [Ps. 5:4-6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%205:4-6&version=nrsv); [Nah. 1:2](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Nah.%201:2&version=nrsv); [Rom. 1:18](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%201:18&version=nrsv); [Eph. 5:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Eph.%205:6&version=nrsv); [Heb. 9:27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Heb.%209:27&version=nrsv) having declared:

“Cursed is everyone who does not observe and obey all the things written in the book of the law.”^[Gal. 3:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gal.%203:10&version=nrsv); [Deut. 27:26](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deut.%2027:26&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 11

**Q. But isn’t God also merciful?**

**A.** God is certainly merciful,^[Ex. 34:6-7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ex.%2034:6-7&version=nrsv); [Ps. 103:8-9](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%20103:8-9&version=nrsv) but also just.^[Ex. 34:7](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ex.%2034:7&version=nrsv); [Deut. 7:9-11](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Deut.%207:9-11&version=nrsv); [Ps. 5:4-6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%205:4-6&version=nrsv); [Heb. 10:30-31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Heb.%2010:30-31&version=nrsv) God’s justice demands that sin, committed against his supreme majesty, be punished with the supreme penalty— eternal punishment of body and soul.^[Matt. 25:35-46](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Matt.%2025:35-46&version=nrsv)
