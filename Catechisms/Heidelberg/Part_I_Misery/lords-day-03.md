## Lord’s Day 3

### Q & A 6

**Q. Did God create people so wicked and perverse?**

**A.** No.  
God created them good^[Gen. 1:31](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%201:31&version=nrsv) and in his own image,^[Gen. 1:26-27](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%201:26-27&version=nrsv)

that is, in true righteousness and holiness,^[Eph. 4:24](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Eph.%204:24&version=nrsv)

so that they might

truly know God their creator,^[Col. 3:10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Col.%203:10&vversion=nrsv)  
love him with all their heart,  
and live with God in eternal happiness,

to praise and glorify him.^[Ps. 8](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%208&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 7

**Q. Then where does this corrupt human nature come from?**

**A.** The fall and disobedience of our first parents,

Adam and Eve, in Paradise.^[Gen. 3](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%203&version=nrsv)

This fall has so poisoned our nature^[Rom. 5:12, 18-19](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Rom.%205:12,%2018-19&version=nrsv)

that we are all conceived and born  
in a sinful condition.^[Ps. 51:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Ps.%2051:5&version=nrsv)

### Q & A 8

**Q. But are we so corrupt that we are totally unable to do any good and inclined toward all evil?**

**A.** Yes,^[Gen. 6:5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%206:5&version=nrsv); [8:21](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Gen.%208:21&version=nrsv); [Job 14:4](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Job%2014:4&version=nrsv); [Isa. 53:6](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Isa.%2053:6&version=nrsv) unless we are born again by the Spirit of God.^[John 3:3-5](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=John%203:3-5&version=nrsv)
